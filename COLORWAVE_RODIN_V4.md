# ColorWave Rodin v4

Speaker tuning goals:
- ViPER/ColorWave as the exclusive session-0 software DSP.
- Keep FS19xx SmartPA calibration and thermal protection untouched.
- More perceived loudness through adaptive Playback Gain rather than fixed preamp.
- Avoid sub-bass headroom waste.
- Stronger punch around the useful phone-speaker bass region.
- Reduced boxiness and restrained presence/detail lift.
- Final limiter at 0.90 for peak control.
- LUFS, MBC, FET, convolver and reverb remain off in the factory speaker profile.

- PGC 65 / 130 / 94
